隱私權政策

色調底片（Toneroll）· 生效日期：2026 年 10 月 22 日

重點

  • 照片只在你的手機上處理，不會上傳，我們也看不到。
  • 使用統計只有在你選擇「分享使用統計」之後才會傳送。不分享也能使用全部功能。
  • 目前的版本不放廣告、不使用廣告 ID。我們不出售或出租任何資料。
  • 付款由 Google Play 處理，我們拿不到你的付款資料。

1. 誰負責這些資料

色調底片（英文名 Toneroll，Google Play 套件名稱 tw.nhho.toneroll）由台灣的個人開發者 nhho.lab 製作和提供，以下稱「我們」。開發者的法定姓名和地址顯示在 Google Play 上本 App 的開發者資訊中。聯絡信箱：nhho.lab@gmail.com。

這份政策說明 App 會處理哪些資料、為什麼處理、存放在哪裡和多久，以及你有哪些權利。它也是依《個人資料保護法》第 8 條給你的告知。

2. 照片和相機

  • App 使用相機權限，只為了顯示取景畫面和拍照。
  • 濾鏡效果都在手機上計算。照片存在手機的「Pictures/Toneroll」資料夾，不會上傳到任何伺服器。
  • 存下的照片會記錄拍攝時間，不記錄位置。App 不要求定位權限。
  • 使用「編輯照片」時，你透過 Android 的相片挑選工具選一張照片。App 只讀取這張照片和它的拍攝時間，並把拍攝時間寫進新存的照片。

3. 只存在手機上的資料

以下資料存在 App 自己的儲存空間，資料本身不會傳給我們：

  • 「我的濾鏡」的名稱和設定。
  • 每台特色相機已經用掉的免費照片張數。
  • 是否已經解鎖 Pro。
  • 你對「分享使用統計」的選擇，以及最近三組統計代碼（見第 4 節）。

如果你分享使用統計，第 4 節列出的內容會包含其中一部分，例如存成「我的濾鏡」時的設定數值（不含名稱）、是否用掉免費照片，以及是否完成解鎖。

如果你開啟了 Android 的 Google 備份，Android 可能會把「我的濾鏡」和免費照片張數備份到你的 Google 帳號，方便換手機時還原。這份備份由 Google 依你的帳號保管，我們看不到。

解除安裝 App 會刪除這些資料，但已經存下的照片會留在手機上。「匯出設定」會把濾鏡設定存成一個檔案，存放位置由你選擇，要不要分享給別人也由你決定。

4. 使用統計（由你選擇）

第一次開啟 App 時，App 會問你要不要「分享使用統計」。兩個選項一樣大，不分享也能使用全部功能。在你回答之前，以及選擇「不要分享」之後，App 都不會傳送使用統計（購買功能本身的運作紀錄見第 5 節）。你隨時可以在「設定 › 隱私」改變選擇。

分享時會傳送的內容

  • 看了哪一台相機或哪一個濾鏡，以及它屬於擴充相機、基礎相機還是我的濾鏡。
  • 存下照片時：用的相機、照片來自拍照還是編輯、形狀（照片比例）、相框、是否加上日期戳記、錄影文字樣式、是否開閃光燈，以及是否用掉一張免費照片。
  • 完成「調整」時：改了哪些設定，以及新的數值（例如曝光 +0.35）。
  • 存成「我的濾鏡」時：它從哪一台內建相機調出來、效果版本，以及設定數值。不包含你取的名稱。
  • 付費頁面：從哪裡打開（例如免費照片用完時）和是哪一台相機、選了哪一種方案，以及是否完成解鎖。
  • Google Analytics 自動記錄的項目：第一次開啟、每次使用的時段和時間長度、App 和系統更新、清除 App 資料和解除安裝 App，以及 Google Analytics 收到的 App 內購買、訂閱續訂、取消和退款（項目、金額和幣別，不含付款資料）。
  • 裝置和安裝資訊：裝置品牌、型號和類型（手機或平板）、系統版本、App 版本、語言和時區，以及 App 是從哪個商店安裝的和 Google Play 提供的安裝來源。
  • 大略位置：國家、地區或城市，由 Google 依網路位址（IP 位址）推估。Google Analytics 不保存 IP 位址。
  • 統計代碼：在這支手機上隨機產生的一組代碼（App 執行個體 ID），讓統計能分辨不同的手機。它和你的姓名、電子郵件或 Google 帳號沒有關聯。Firebase 另外會使用一組隨機的安裝 ID 來提供服務。

永遠不會傳送的內容

照片和影像內容、照片的拍攝資訊和位置、你輸入的文字（例如濾鏡名稱）、「我的濾鏡」的內部代號、你的 Google 帳號、電子郵件、電話號碼、聯絡人，以及廣告 ID。

App 自己送出的每一筆統計，都會先檢查內容，只允許上面列出的項目和數值。Google Analytics 自動記錄的項目由 Google 的程式庫直接傳送，不經過這個檢查。

用途和依據

我們用這些資料了解大家喜歡哪些相機和設定、哪些地方不好用，以改善 App、決定之後做哪些相機，並了解付費頁面是否清楚。我們不會用它投放廣告或建立個人檔案，也不會出售。

我們依你的同意蒐集使用統計。不分享不會影響任何功能。

誰處理這些資料

使用統計透過 Google LLC 提供的 Google Analytics for Firebase 蒐集。Google 依它和我們之間的資料處理條款，替我們處理這些資料。我們關閉了 Google Analytics 的所有資料共用選項和 Google 信號，也沒有連結任何廣告服務。想進一步了解，請看Google 如何運用來自採用 Google 服務的網站和應用程式的資訊。

資料存放在 Google 的伺服器，可能位在台灣以外的國家或地區，例如美國。

保存多久

和統計代碼連在一起的詳細紀錄保存 2 個月，之後由 Google 自動刪除。Google 大約每個月執行一次刪除，所以實際上可能多留幾週。不含統計代碼的彙總數字（例如「某台相機這個月存了 500 張照片」）會在 App 提供期間繼續保存。

Firebase 的安裝 ID 不受 2 個月的設定限制：在你分享期間由 Google 保存；你關閉分享時，App 會請 Google 刪除它；這支手機上的 App 大約 270 天沒有使用它時，Google 也會自動刪除。

停止分享

在「設定 › 隱私」關閉「分享使用統計」後，App 會停止傳送，並換掉這支手機的統計代碼，之後的統計無法和之前的連在一起；再開啟時會使用新的代碼。舊的代碼只留在「設定 › 隱私」的清單裡（最近三組），方便你要求刪除，不會再用來傳送資料。已經傳送的資料會依上面「保存多久」說明的期限刪除，或依你的要求提早刪除。

App 和 Google 之間的傳輸都經過加密（HTTPS）。第 3 節的資料存在 App 的私人空間，其他 App 無法讀取。存下的照片在手機共用的「Pictures/Toneroll」資料夾，有相片存取權限的其他 App（例如相簿）可以讀取；匯出的設定檔存在你選擇的位置。只有開發者本人能進入統計和訂單的後台。

Privacy Policy

Toneroll (色調底片) · Effective date: 22 October 2026

This English version is a translation. If the two versions differ, the Chinese version applies. The app’s screens are in Chinese only, so the Chinese label is shown in brackets after each English name.

In short

  • Your photos are processed only on your phone. They are never uploaded, and we never see them.
  • Usage statistics are sent only after you choose “Share usage statistics” (分享使用統計). Every feature works without sharing.
  • The current version has no ads and does not use the advertising ID. We never sell or rent any data.
  • Google Play handles payment. We never receive your payment details.

1. Who is responsible for the data

Toneroll (色調底片, Google Play package name tw.nhho.toneroll) is made and provided by nhho.lab, an individual developer in Taiwan (“we”). The developer’s legal name and address are shown in this app’s developer details on Google Play. Contact: nhho.lab@gmail.com.

This policy explains what data the app handles, why, where it is kept and for how long, and what your rights are. It is also the notice required by Article 8 of Taiwan’s Personal Data Protection Act.

2. Photos and the camera

  • The app uses the camera permission only to show the viewfinder and take photos.
  • All filter effects are computed on your phone. Photos are saved in the phone’s “Pictures/Toneroll” folder and are never uploaded to any server.
  • Saved photos record the time they were taken, but not the location. The app does not ask for location permission.
  • When you use “Edit photo” (編輯照片), you choose one photo with Android’s photo picker. The app reads only that photo and the time it was taken, and writes that time into the new photo it saves.

3. Data that stays on your phone

The following data is kept in the app’s own storage. The data itself is not sent to us:

  • The names and settings of My filters (我的濾鏡).
  • How many free photos you have used with each featured camera.
  • Whether Pro is unlocked.
  • Your answer about sharing usage statistics, and your last three statistics IDs (see section 4).

If you share usage statistics, the items in section 4 include part of this data, such as the setting values when you save to My filters (never the name), whether a free photo was used, and whether Pro was unlocked.

If Android’s Google backup is on, Android may back up My filters and the free photo counts to your Google Account, so they can be restored on a new phone. Google keeps this backup under your account, and we cannot see it.

Uninstalling the app deletes this data, but the photos you saved stay on your phone. “Export settings” (匯出設定) saves a filter’s settings as a file in a place you choose. Whether you share that file is up to you.

4. Usage statistics (your choice)

The first time you open the app, it asks whether you want to share usage statistics. The two answers are the same size, and every feature works without sharing. Before you answer, and after you choose “Don’t share” (不要分享), the app sends no usage statistics (for the logs of the purchase feature itself, see section 5). You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy (設定 › 隱私).

What is sent when you share

  • Which camera or filter you looked at, and whether it is an extra camera (擴充相機), a base camera (基礎相機) or one of My filters.
  • When you save a photo: the camera used, whether the photo came from the camera or the editor, the shape (形狀, the aspect ratio), the frame (相框), whether the date stamp (日期戳記) is on, the on-screen video text style (錄影文字), whether the flash was on, and whether a free photo was used.
  • When you finish “Adjust” (調整): which settings you changed and their new values (for example, exposure +0.35).
  • When you save to My filters: the built-in camera it was made from, the effect version, and the setting values. Never the name you gave it.
  • The paywall: where it was opened from (for example, when the free photos ran out) and for which camera, which plan you chose, and whether Pro was unlocked.
  • Events that Google Analytics records automatically: first open, when and how long you use the app, app and system updates, clearing the app’s data and uninstalling the app, and any in-app purchases, subscription renewals, cancellations and refunds that Google Analytics receives (the item, amount and currency, never payment details).
  • Device and install details: device brand, model and type (phone or tablet), system version, app version, language and time zone, and which app store the app was installed from, with the install source that Google Play reports.
  • Approximate location: country, region or city, estimated by Google from the network (IP) address. Google Analytics does not store IP addresses.
  • A statistics ID: a random code created on this phone (the app instance ID), so the statistics can tell phones apart. It is not linked to your name, email address or Google Account. Firebase also uses a random installation ID to provide its service.

What is never sent

Photos and image content, photo details and location, text you type (such as filter names), the internal IDs of My filters, your Google Account, email address, phone number, contacts, and the advertising ID.

Before the app sends each of its own events, it checks it and allows only the items and values listed above. Events that Google Analytics records automatically are sent directly by Google’s library and do not go through this check.

Purpose and legal basis

We use this data to learn which cameras and settings people like and what is hard to use, so we can improve the app, decide which cameras to make next, and check that the paywall is clear. We never use it for advertising or to build a profile of you, and we never sell it.

We collect usage statistics with your consent. Not sharing does not affect any feature.

Who processes the data

Usage statistics are collected with Google Analytics for Firebase, a service of Google LLC. Google processes the data for us under its data processing terms with us. We have turned off all Google Analytics data sharing options and Google signals, and the app is not linked to any advertising service. To learn more, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

The data is stored on Google’s servers, which may be outside Taiwan, for example in the United States.

How long it is kept

Detailed records linked to a statistics ID are kept for 2 months, then Google deletes them automatically. Google runs this deletion about once a month, so records may stay a few weeks longer. Totals without a statistics ID (for example, “500 photos were saved with a camera this month”) are kept while the app is offered.

The Firebase installation ID is not covered by the 2-month setting. Google keeps it while you share. When you turn sharing off, the app asks Google to delete it, and Google also deletes it automatically after the app on this phone has not used it for about 270 days.

Stopping sharing

When you turn off “Share usage statistics” (分享使用統計) in Settings › Privacy (設定 › 隱私), the app stops sending and replaces the statistics ID on this phone, so later statistics cannot be joined to earlier ones. A new ID is used if you turn sharing on again. The old IDs (the last three) stay only in the list in Settings › Privacy, so you can ask us to delete their data. They are never used to send data again. Data already sent is deleted as described in “How long it is kept” above, or earlier if you ask.

5. Purchases

  • Google Play handles Pro purchases. Only Google receives your payment details (such as your card number), under the Google Privacy Policy.
  • The app asks Google Play on your phone for your purchase status (the item, its state and the purchase token) to unlock Pro. The result is kept on your phone. Only if you share usage statistics are the plan you chose and whether Pro was unlocked sent, as section 4 describes.
  • The app includes the Google Play Billing Library, made by Google, for buying and restoring purchases. From the first time you open the app, whether or not you share usage statistics or buy anything, this library sends its own logs to Google. They include the library version, the app’s package name, the result code and message of each request to Google Play, device brand, manufacturer and model, system version and the full system build name (build fingerprint), language, time zone, country, mobile carrier code, network type, and a random number that is new each time the app starts. Google handles these logs under the Google Privacy Policy. We cannot see them or turn them off.
  • Google Play shows us order details in the developer console, such as the order number, item, amount, date and country or region. We use them only for refunds, support and the financial records the law requires, and keep them as long as the law requires. This data is kept on Google’s servers, which may be outside Taiwan.

6. When you email us

We receive your email address and your message, and use them only to reply to you. Emails are kept in Gmail, an email service of Google, whose servers may be outside Taiwan. After your question is settled, we keep the email for at most 12 months, then delete it.

7. Statistics from Google Play

Google Play gives us summary reports that do not identify you, such as install counts, ratings, and crash and performance reports (only if you allow your phone to share usage and diagnostics data with Google). Google handles these reports under its own policies.

8. The current version

  • No ads, and no advertising ID.
  • No account to sign up for.
  • Only the camera permission: no location, contacts or microphone permission.

Later versions may add ads or video with sound. Before they do, we will update this policy to say what data is handled and who receives it. When video needs the microphone, Android will ask you first.

In every version, we never sell or rent any data.

9. Security

Data sent between the app and Google is encrypted (HTTPS). The data in section 3 is kept in the app’s private storage, which other apps cannot read. Saved photos go to the phone’s shared “Pictures/Toneroll” folder, which other apps with photo access (such as your gallery) can read. Exported settings files go where you choose. Only the developer can open the statistics and order consoles.

10. Your rights

Under Article 3 of Taiwan’s Personal Data Protection Act, for the data we hold about you, you may ask whether we hold it and ask to see it, ask for a copy, ask us to add to or correct it, ask us to stop collecting, processing or using it, and ask us to delete it.

Email nhho.lab@gmail.com with the statistics ID shown in Settings › Privacy (設定 › 隱私). We do not know which ID belongs to whom, so we need the ID to find your data. We will not ask for identity documents. We reply to requests to check, see or copy data within 15 days, and handle requests to add, correct, stop or delete within 30 days. If we need more time, we will tell you why.

11. Children

The app is not made for children under 13. If you believe a child has sent usage statistics through the app, please email us and we will delete them.

12. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, we update the effective date on this page. If usage statistics are going to include new kinds of data, the app will ask you again and send them only after you agree.
