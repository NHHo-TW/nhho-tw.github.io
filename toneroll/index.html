常見問題 FAQ

在別的手機買過一次買斷，按「恢復購買」卻沒有解鎖？

先確認這支手機的 Google Play 用的是購買時的 Google 帳號。新手機的 Google Play 有時還沒有一次買斷的紀錄，所以「恢復購買」找不到。這時請在 App 的「解鎖全部相機」點「一次買斷」：Google Play 會顯示「你已擁有這項商品」，關掉後就會解鎖。如果 Google Play 要你付款，表示這個帳號沒有買過，請按返回取消，再確認 Google Play 用的帳號。訂閱不受影響，同一個帳號打開 App 就會解鎖。

I bought the one-time purchase on another phone, but “Restore purchase” does not unlock it.

First check that Google Play on this phone uses the Google Account you bought it with. Google Play on a new phone sometimes does not have the one-time purchase yet, so “Restore purchase” (恢復購買) cannot find it. In the app, open “Unlock all cameras” (解鎖全部相機) and tap “One-time purchase” (一次買斷): Google Play says you already own the item; close it and Pro unlocks. If Google Play asks you to pay, this account has not bought it: go back without paying and check which account Google Play uses. Subscriptions are not affected: they unlock when you open the app with the same account.
